About Vacuum Flask & Bottles Manufacturing

Vacuum flask and insulated bottle manufacturing involves several advanced industrial processes. Stainless steel sheets are cut, formed, welded and assembled to produce durable bottles with excellent thermal insulation.

The manufacturing process includes hydraulic forming, deep drawing, necking, welding, vacuum evacuation, polishing, coating, testing and final packaging.

Modern machinery ensures product quality, dimensional accuracy, durability and efficient production.

Machines Used in Manufacturing

Complete range of machinery used in vacuum flask and bottle production.

1. Sheet Cutting Machine

Used to cut stainless steel sheets into required sizes for inner and outer bottle bodies.

2. Deep Drawing Hydraulic Press

Forms stainless steel sheets into cylindrical shapes for bottle manufacturing.

3. Hydraulic Forming Machine

Shapes bottle bodies, shoulders, bases and other stainless steel components.

4. Necking Machine

Reduces and shapes the bottle neck to achieve the required opening diameter.

5. Welding Machine

Joins stainless steel components of inner and outer bottle bodies.

6. Vacuum Pumping Machine

Removes air from the space between the inner and outer walls to create vacuum insulation.

7. Vacuum Brazing / Sealing Furnace

Used in suitable manufacturing processes for joining or sealing components under controlled conditions.

8. Polishing Machine

Provides a smooth, shiny and attractive finish to the bottle surface.

9. Powder Coating / Spray Painting Machine

Applies decorative and protective coatings to the external bottle surface.

10. Leak Testing Machine

Checks bottles for leakage and defective joints.

11. Thermal Insulation Testing Machine

Tests the ability of the flask to maintain liquid temperature.

12. Bottle Assembly Machine

Fits caps, lids, handles, seals and other accessories.
