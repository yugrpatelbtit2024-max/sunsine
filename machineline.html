About Cookware Forming Presses

Cookware forming presses are industrial machines used to shape flat sheets of stainless steel, aluminium and other suitable metals into cookware products such as pots, pans, pressure cookers and lids.

These machines use controlled pressing force and specially designed dies to form metal into the required shape. Depending on the application, hydraulic or servo-hydraulic systems can provide accurate control of the forming process.

Properly configured forming presses help manufacturers achieve consistent product dimensions, reliable production quality and efficient material usage.

Key Features

  • High-Precision Forming: Helps maintain consistent cookware dimensions.
  • Heavy-Duty Construction: Designed for demanding industrial production.
  • Hydraulic Technology: Provides controlled force during forming.
  • Custom Tooling: Dies can be designed for different cookware shapes.
  • Production Flexibility: Supports different cookware sizes and designs.

Applications

Cookware forming presses can be used to manufacture stainless steel pots, frying pans, stockpots, pressure cooker bodies, cookware lids and other metal kitchenware components.
