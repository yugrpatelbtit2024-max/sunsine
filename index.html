About Manufacturing Machinery

Sun-Sine Corporation specializes in the engineering, fabrication, and commissioning of heavy industrial machinery used to manufacture premium kitchenware, stainless steel cookware, and domestic appliances.

From single-machine installations to complete turnkey automated factories, our equipment is built with high-torque servo hydraulics, Siemens S7-1500 PLC control, and Rexroth proportional valves—reducing cycle time by up to 42% and material scrap by 28%.

  • ✓ Multi-Action Deep Drawing Presses (50T–3500T): Precision shaping for stockpots, pressure cookers, and double-bowl kitchen sinks.
  • ✓ CNC Edge Trimming & Beading Lathes: High-speed height cutting, rim curling, and automated off-cut scrap chopping.
  • ✓ Bottom Impact Bonding Presses: 2500T kinetic friction press for induction-ready multi-ply sandwich base cookware.
  • ✓ Robotic Buffing & Non-Stick Coating Lines: 6-axis mirror polishing and 36-meter 420°C infrared PTFE/ceramic curing tunnels.
  • ✓ Appliance Injection Molding & Stamping: Casing molding for blenders, air fryers, electric kettles, and bakelite handles.
